---
name: Dragon Tiger fairness
description: Outcome-integrity boundary for Dragon Tiger configuration and risk controls
---

Dragon Tiger result selection must remain independent of wager totals, user tier, or onboarding status. Risk controls belong before bet acceptance, and onboarding rewards must be transparent, side-independent, and ledger-auditable.

**Why:** Changing card generation after seeing wagers would create hidden odds and undermine player trust and financial/regulatory safety.

**How to apply:** Keep any payout or onboarding configuration separate from the secure random result generator; use it only for pre-bet exposure checks or disclosed, idempotent rewards.
